Welcome to Scholi — a simple calendar for parents, teachers and schools.
In this short demo I’ll show you how to sign in, find your child’s activities, and how teachers can create events.

First, sign in as a parent using the seeded credentials: aisha.khan@example.com, password ParentPass123!
You’ll land on the dashboard which shows upcoming activities across your children. Use the child chips to filter, and tap any event to see details, attachments and reminder options.

Teachers can create activities using the Create Activity screen — enter title, start and end times, assign to children or a class, and publish. The event appears immediately on the calendar for parents.

You can sync events to your personal calendar and set notification lead times in settings. That’s Scholi — one place for your child’s schedule. Thank you for trying Scholi!
